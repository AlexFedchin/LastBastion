HOW YOU LOSE

The dead pile up against the wall. Every body pressed against a section raises the pile. When a pile reaches the top of the wall, they climb over — and each one that makes it costs a survivor. When the survivors are gone, so is Bastion-7.

Watch the WALL PRESSURE map. Look left and right along the wall faces and cut the piles down. Napalm and grenades are made for the wall base.

HOW YOU LAST

Survive until dawn. Every kill earns supplies; spend them at dawn on the gun, the battery, the wall and the men on it. Ten nights. Then we'll see.

Bloaters burst and take their neighbours with them — but if one reaches the wall, its acid melts a ramp the rest can climb. Brutes weigh three men on a pile. Runners are exactly what they sound like. Flares light targets up: lit zombies take +25% damage.

You start with the fifty and two gunners on the wall. Everything else — napalm, grenades, the battery, flares, the gunship, the bomber — comes up the road over the first nights. Listen to the radio when it does.

Every night brings something of its own — people to bring home, a crate to hold, things far bigger than a brute. Listen to the radio and watch the OBJECTIVE panel.
