HOW YOU LOSE

The dead pile up against the wall. Once a mound starts, more of them come to it, and every body on it raises it. When a mound reaches the top of the wall, they climb over — and each one that makes it costs a survivor. When the survivors are gone, so is Bastion-7.

Watch the WALL PRESSURE map. Look left and right along the wall faces and cut the piles down. Molotovs and grenades are made for the wall base.

HOW YOU LAST

Survive until dawn. Every kill earns supplies; spend them at dawn on the gun, the battery, the wall and the men on it. Ten nights, then the extraction flight comes, but only if there are at least fifteen survivors left to carry out. Keep your people alive.

Bloaters burst and take their neighbours with them — but if one reaches the wall, its acid melts a ramp the rest can climb. Brutes weigh three men on a pile. Runners are exactly what they sound like.

You start with the fifty and two gunners on the wall. Everything else — Molotovs, grenades, the battery, the gunship, the bomber — comes up the road over the first nights. Listen to the radio when it does.

Every night brings something of its own — people to bring home, a crate to hold, things far bigger than a brute. Listen to the radio and watch the OBJECTIVE panel.
